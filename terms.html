What Pubvera is

Pubvera is a set of eight tools that search public, open scientific and regulatory data — published literature, clinical trial registries, medical device records, retraction notices, drug recalls and research grants. Some tools can additionally send what they found to an AI model, which writes a summary or a verdict.

Pubvera is operated by an individual developer based in Romania. It is not a company, a laboratory, or a medical or legal service.

What you must not rely on it for

Not medical, legal or financial advice. Nothing produced here is a diagnosis, a treatment recommendation, a regulatory determination, or advice on what to take, prescribe, buy, avoid or invest in. If a health decision is involved, speak to a qualified professional. Do not change a treatment on the strength of anything on this site.

The AI can be wrong, and it can be wrong confidently. AI-written summaries have been observed to attribute studies to the wrong authors and to reach different conclusions when asked the identical question twice. Treat every AI verdict as a starting point, and check it against the underlying sources — which each tool links to for exactly this reason.

Absence of a record is not evidence of absence. If a search finds no recall, no retraction or no trial, that means nothing matching was found in the data available at that moment. It does not prove none exists.

The underlying data comes from third-party sources such as OpenAlex, PubMed, the FDA and clinical trial registries. Pubvera passes on what they publish and cannot guarantee that it is complete, current or correct.
